## Beta retention policy selected

The policy preserves the Phase 0 candidates and must be implemented before public deployment:

| Data family | Retention | Enforcement requirement |
|---|---:|---|
| Raw logs, raw metrics, native traces, native service map | 7 days | Event-date indexes and tested ISM/delete behavior; reject expired replay rather than routing it into current data. |
| Service metric buckets, native AD results, normalized anomalies | 30 days | Keep native and normalized horizons aligned; never expire unprocessed/failed backlog silently. |
| Resolved incidents, evidence, terminal investigations | 90 days | Delete only after resolution/finish and by reference reachability; retain active incidents/jobs and every evidence item referenced by a retained bundle/report. |
| Worker cursors, bootstrap marker, active detector registrations | No automatic expiry | Remove only superseded/versioned records through an explicit migration. |

Accepted Phase 5 traffic produced approximately 77–120 business requests per minute during bounded scenarios. Phase 9 measured 58,829 stored spans at 18.5 MiB, 47,957 raw metric documents at approximately 21.3 MiB across two daily indexes, and 10,445 log documents at approximately 2.6 MiB across two daily indexes. This is a point-in-time development-volume measurement, not bytes/day or a capacity claim. Phase 10 must measure a known-duration workload before sizing EC2 storage. Use the 7/30/90-day policy as the starting envelope.

## Phase 10 security gate

Phase 10 may deploy only after the critical/high deployment blockers above are closed or explicitly rejected by the owner. The minimum beta edge is a TLS reverse proxy serving frontend/API, secret-backed authentication, secure cookies or equivalent proxy credentials, request-size/rate limits, and security-group rules that expose only 443 plus narrowly sourced administration. OpenSearch Dashboards remains private and is reached only through an authenticated administrative tunnel if required.
